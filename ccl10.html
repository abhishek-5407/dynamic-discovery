ABOUT THE PROPERTY

What is Content Creator League?

Content Creator League (CCL) Season 1 is India's premier Tennis Ball T10 cricket tournament, designed exclusively for digital content creators and influencers.

Organised and managed by Dynamic Discovery, CCL brings social media personalities from across the country together to compete, collaborate, entertain and inspire through the spirit of cricket.

It is more than a sporting event — it is a celebration of digital influence, entertainment, sportsmanship and brand collaboration.
